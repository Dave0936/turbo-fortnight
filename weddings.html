One wedding at a time

There is no second party in the next room and no clock ticking on your evening. For the day you have booked, Coul House and its eight acres belong to you and the people you have asked to be there.

The Ceremony

Marry beneath the ornate ceiling, or with the mountains behind you

Coul House is licensed for civil ceremonies, so you can marry here rather than travel between two places on the one day you would rather not be in a car.

  • The Octagonal room — eight walls, an eighteen-foot ornate ceiling, a fireplace, and three ceiling-to-floor windows onto Strathconon
  • On the lawn — with the mature woodland and the hills as your backdrop
  • Among the trees — for something quieter and more sheltered
  • Church weddings — we are glad to host the reception if you are marrying nearby

How the Day Runs

Unhurried, and shaped around you

The morning

Getting ready in your rooms, with breakfast brought up and the grounds quiet before anyone arrives.

The ceremony

In the Octagonal room, on the lawn or under the trees — and we will have a wet-weather plan ready either way.

Dinner

Drinks and photographs in the grounds, then a long table and a menu you have chosen with our kitchen.

The evening

A ceilidh, the bar, the fire in the drawing room, and no need for anybody to find a taxi.

Everybody Stays

The best part is the morning after

Twenty-one bedrooms means your family and closest friends sleep in the same house you married in. Nobody leaves early to catch a lift. The party ends when it ends.

Then breakfast, together, in the room you danced in the night before — which is, by common agreement, the part people remember.

  • Twenty-one en suite bedrooms, held for your guests
  • Dogs are welcome, if yours is part of the day
  • Free parking in the grounds for everyone
  • Twenty-five minutes from Inverness and its airport
