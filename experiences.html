Experiences · Ross-shire

The Highlands begin
at the end of the drive

Forest trails from the grounds, a waterfall two miles off, and the whole north-west within a day's drive — with a fire to come back to.

Well placed, and quietly so

Coul House sits above Contin on the North Coast 500, twenty-five minutes north of Inverness. Far enough out that the nights are properly dark; close enough in that nothing worth seeing is a chore to reach.

Within Three Miles

On the doorstep

2 miles · Achilty Forest

Rogie Falls

The Blackwater drops through a rocky gorge with a suspension bridge over it and a fish ladder alongside. Come in summer and you may watch salmon leaping it. There are forest walks, viewpoints and mountain-bike trails through the same woods — and a path to them from our own grounds.

3 miles · Victorian spa village

Strathpeffer

A Victorian spa village that has kept its pump room, its pavilion and its slightly improbable grandeur. There are shops worth an hour, a theatre, and enough tearooms to make an afternoon of it.

3 miles · Brahan Estate

Brahan & the lochs

River walks and fishing on the Brahan Estate, with Loch Achilty and Loch Ussie both inside five miles — quiet water, and the sort of walk that suits a morning rather than a whole day.

Hills & Glens

Ben Wyvis, Strathconon, and a great deal of quiet

Ben Wyvis rises to the north-east — a long, whale-backed Munro and a serious but very manageable day out. Behind the house, Strathconon runs west into some of the emptiest country in Ross-shire, a single-track road following the river past lochs and old pinewood until it simply stops.

Tell us the night before and the kitchen will put up something to take with you. Tell us when you expect to be back and we will have tea waiting, or come looking.

  • Forest trails leaving directly from the grounds
  • Ben Wyvis for a full hill day; Strathconon for a slow drive
  • Boots dried overnight, and no comment on the state of them
Malt

Distilleries, near and rather nearer

Glen Ord sits a short drive south at Muir of Ord, making one of the Highlands' more underrated malts and running tours worth the detour. Beyond it, Speyside is a comfortable day, and the north coast distilleries make a fine excuse to keep driving.

Come back and compare notes at our own bar, where the shelves are well stocked and nobody will rush your second dram.

The North Coast 500

The right place to start, or to break the drive

Coul House is on the route. Whether you are setting out or coming back down, this is a considerably better night than most of what the road offers.

Inverness

25 minutes. The airport, the trains, and the start of the loop.

Ullapool

Just over an hour west, for the Stornoway ferry and the sea.

Torridon & Applecross

A long, spectacular day, and worth every mile of it.

Skye

Reachable in a day, though we would rather you gave it two.

Or Simply Stay Put

Eight acres, and no particular obligation

Not every day has to be an expedition. There are eight acres of lawn and mature woodland here, a mini golf course, a play area for children, and a patio that catches the afternoon sun.

Some of our best-reviewed days involve a book, a chair, and going no further than the tree line.

  • Woodland and lawn walks within the grounds
  • Mini golf and a children's play area
  • Dogs off the lead among the trees
  • An open fire in the drawing room when the weather turns

Make a few days of it

There is more here than one night allows. Stay two or three, and let us point you at the walks worth doing in the weather you actually get.
