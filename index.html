Chapter 01 — The Welcome

Twenty years on, and still very much a family home.

We are the proud, and very hands-on, owners of this country house hotel — working alongside a fabulous team. The house sits peacefully in the hills above Contin, framed by mature trees and gardens lovingly created over two decades.

We take great pride in delicious food, a considered wine list, a well-stocked bar and, above all, a warm and genuine Highland welcome.

Chapter 02 — The Table

Cuisine

Creative cuisine, delicious dining.

Everything from scratch — from ice cream to pastry and all between. Local chef Garry Kenley prides himself on traditional craft: steaks, venison, hare, halibut, hand-dived scallops and home-cured salmon.
