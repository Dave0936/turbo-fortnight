Chapter 06 — The Location

The Highlands, right on the doorstep.

A perfect base for adventure, or none at all.

Coul House sits in the wee village of Contin, near Strathpeffer — a gateway to lochs, glens, castles and coast. Walk the falls before breakfast, chase a Munro, or simply sink into a fireside chair. The choice, gloriously, is yours.

Finding us

Closer than you think.

By car

Just off the A835, 25 minutes north-west of Inverness. Ample parking at the door.

By train

The Far North Line stops at Garve, a short hop away; Inverness station is 25 minutes.

By air

Inverness Airport is around 40 minutes, with direct flights across the UK and beyond.
