Services

Five things we do, all with our own crews

Nothing is subcontracted. The person who quotes the job is the person who answers the phone from site.

01
Rotary drilling

Open hole and rotary core through rock and old mineworkings, to 250 metres, on tracked and lorry mounted rigs that reach restricted sites.
02
Soil boring

Window sampling and cable percussion through made ground and glacial till, with in situ testing recorded as we go.
03
Environmental assessments

Phase 1 and Phase 2 site investigation, contaminated land sampling, gas and groundwater monitoring wells for planning conditions.
04
Geotechnical appraisals

Boreholes, logging and laboratory scheduling to BS 5930, written up as a factual report your structural engineer can design straight from.
05
Geothermal installations

Closed loop ground source arrays sized with your heat pump designer, then drilled, grouted, pressure tested and signed off.
The ground under a site is the one variable nobody can negotiate with. So we go and look.
