What we put in the ground

Five services, one crew, one point of contact. Ordered by the depth they usually work at.

0 – 10 m
Environmental assessments
Contaminated land sampling, gas and groundwater monitoring wells, Phase 1 and Phase 2 site investigation for planning conditions.
0 – 15 m
Soil boring
Window sampling and cable percussion for made ground, glacial till and the soft stuff above rockhead. In situ testing as we go.
5 – 60 m
Geotechnical appraisals
Boreholes, logging and lab scheduling that answer the foundation question: what will bear the load, and at what depth.
80 – 200 m

to 250 m
Rotary drilling
