The small print

Offers are subject to availability and to the rooms allocated to each rate. Bank holidays, Christmas, New Year and event weekends may be excluded. Send us your dates and we will tell you plainly what applies.

All rates include full Highland breakfast, Wi-Fi, parking and VAT. Prices vary by season, room type and how far ahead you book. Offers cannot normally be combined with one another.

We will confirm the deposit and cancellation terms that apply to your booking in writing before you commit to anything. If your plans change, talk to us early — we are generally able to help.

Dogs are welcome on any of these breaks, in every bedroom and all public rooms except the restaurant, at £7.50 per dog per night.
