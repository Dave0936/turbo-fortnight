Good to know

We will confirm current times with you when you book. Arriving early or leaving late is usually possible — do ask, and we will hold your luggage and pour you a coffee in the meantime.

Dogs are welcome in every bedroom and throughout the public rooms, with the single exception of the restaurant. There is a charge of £7.50 per dog per night. There are forest trails straight from the grounds, so they will sleep well too.

Children are welcome. There is a play area and a mini golf course in the grounds, and the kitchen is happy to cook smaller portions at an earlier sitting. Please tell us ages when you book so we can put you in the right room.

The drawing room, bar and Octagonal Restaurant are all on the ground floor. The house is Georgian, so bedrooms on the upper floors are reached by stairs. Please call us before booking and we will talk you through which rooms will suit.
