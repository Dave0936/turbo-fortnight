Getting Here

Twenty-five minutes from Inverness

Coul House stands on rising ground just outside the village of Contin, a little off the A835 as it runs north-west towards Ullapool. There is free parking in the grounds.

By car

From Inverness, take the A9 north over the Kessock Bridge, then the A835 west through Maryburgh and Marybank to Contin — around twenty-five minutes in all. Coul House is signed on the right as you reach the village.

By air

Inverness Airport is roughly forty minutes away, with flights from across the UK. We can arrange a transfer if you let us know your flight when you book.

By rail

Inverness is the nearest mainline station, around half an hour away. Dingwall, on the Far North Line, is closer still. Tell us your train and we will help you sort the last leg.

Satnav: IV14 9ES will bring you to the village; follow the hotel signs from there.
